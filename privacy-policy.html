Privacy Policy

Undercover: Who's the Mole? · Bored Developer Studios · Effective November 18, 2026

This policy explains what information the Android app Undercover: Who's the Mole? ("the app") uses, why, and the choices you have. The app is made by Bored Developer Studios ("we", "us").

The short version

  • You don't create an account. We never ask for your name, email or phone number.
  • Player names, settings and game progress are saved only on your phone.
  • Free players see ads from Google AdMob, which uses device identifiers to show and measure ads.
  • Subscriptions are handled by Google Play. We never see your card or payment details.
  • We use Google Firebase to understand how the app is used and to switch features on or off.

1. Information stored only on your device

Names you type for players, your chosen theme, sound and vibration settings, unlocked word packs, game counters and your subscription status are stored locally on your phone. We do not receive this information. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage deletes it.

3. Personalised ads and your choices

  • If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before showing personalised ads. You can change your choice any time from the app's Settings.
  • On any Android phone you can delete or reset your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  • Subscribing to Premium removes all ads.

4. What we don't do

We don't sell your personal information. We don't collect your contacts, photos, microphone, precise location or any information that directly identifies you.

5. Children

The app is intended for people aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided information, contact us and we will help remove it.

6. Keeping and deleting data

Data on your device stays until you uninstall the app or clear its storage. Analytics data is kept by Google for up to 14 months. Purchase records are kept as long as needed to provide your subscription and meet legal and tax obligations. To ask us to delete data linked to your app user ID (for example purchase records held by RevenueCat), email us at the address below.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR, India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act or California law), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, or to object to its use. Email us and we will respond within 30 days.

8. Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top. Significant changes will also be mentioned in the app.
